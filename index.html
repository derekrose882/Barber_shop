Our story

One chair, a stereo, and a simple idea.

Marco Reyes opened Palm & Blade in 2014 with one chair, a secondhand stereo, and a simple idea: a barbershop should feel like a backyard on a Sunday.

Twelve years later there are four chairs, the stereo still works, and the fridge always has cold water. Come in for the cut. Stay for the conversation.
